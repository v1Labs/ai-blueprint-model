# style.md guidance

## Purpose

Define the visual, tonal, and brand direction for the asset.

## Recommended sections

- tone and voice
- color or brand system
- typography or hierarchy guidance
- layout philosophy
- consistency rules across variants

## Authoring guidance

- keep stylistic expectations separate from factual content requirements
- describe reusable visual patterns instead of one-off formatting trivia
- capture brand consistency rules that reviewers can recognize

## Common pitfalls

- using style guidance to smuggle in new content requirements
- over-specifying visual details that do not matter to the workflow
