# constraints.md guidance

## Purpose

Define the non-negotiable rules and strong recommendations that apply across the asset blueprint.

## Recommended sections

- `MUST`
- `SHOULD`
- `MAY`

## Authoring guidance

- use normative language consistently
- keep structural, compliance, and factual rules explicit
- reserve stylistic preferences for `style.md` unless they are truly mandatory

## Common pitfalls

- encoding brand preferences as hard requirements without justification
- scattering mandatory rules across multiple files
