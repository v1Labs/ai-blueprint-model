# outputs.md guidance

## Purpose

Describe the asset or asset set the blueprint should produce.

## Recommended sections

- primary deliverable
- format or channel variants
- packaging expectations
- export or handoff notes

## Authoring guidance

- describe the final artifact in operationally useful terms
- document variant relationships when multiple channels or formats are involved
- keep delivery expectations separate from content and style guidance

## Common pitfalls

- mixing output packaging with review criteria
- duplicating content rules that belong in `content.md`
