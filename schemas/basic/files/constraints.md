# constraints.md guidance

## Purpose

Define the rules that govern blueprint execution.

## Recommended sections

- `MUST`
- `SHOULD`
- `MAY`

## Authoring guidance

- use normative language consistently
- keep `MUST` constraints specific enough to review or validate
- use `SHOULD` for strong guidance that allows justified exceptions
- keep optional guidance clearly separate from required behavior

## Common pitfalls

- mixing non-negotiable rules and preferences together
- writing vague constraints that cannot be reviewed
